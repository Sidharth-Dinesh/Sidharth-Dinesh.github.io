I am a Systems Analyst at Arizona State University’s W. P. Carey School of Business, where I work on undergraduate-advising software, database integrations, and data applications for faculty and staff. My background combines computer science with electronics and edge computing.

I am interested in spatial and spatiotemporal data systems, particularly how we process and explore geographic data to understand movement, cities, and environmental change. My interests include spatial analysis, interactive mapping, and the systems that make large geographic datasets useful.

Research interests

  • Spatial and spatiotemporal data systems. Spatial data management and scalable geocomputation, with an interest in trajectories, streaming observations, and data that change over time.
  • Spatial analysis and interactive mapping. Spatial statistics and geovisualization for finding geographic patterns and exploring them through linked maps and timelines.
  • Urban and environmental applications. Human mobility, pollution, climate, and disaster response. I am interested in combining geographic data sources and using geospatial AI to help investigate these problems.
